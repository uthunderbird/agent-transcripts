## Summary

What changes, in a few sentences?

## Problem

What concrete ambiguity, defect, or missing capability exists?

## Proposed normative change

Describe the rule and list every normative file changed in the same pull
request. The prospective file diff is part of the proposal.

## Examples

Give at least one accepted example and, when relevant, one rejected or boundary
example.

## Compatibility

Explain why the selected class applies, which existing artifacts are affected,
and what version or migration consequence follows.

## Core and profiles

State whether the change belongs to the product-neutral core, a named profile,
or governance.

## Alternatives considered

What simpler designs were considered, and why were they rejected?

## Future tooling impact

What would a future validator, compiler, runner, scorer, judge, or adapter need
to do? This section does not authorize or claim that tooling.

## Local polish

List 1–5 cold rounds. For each round record the reviewed commit SHA, result, and
a committed summary of every P0/P1 with stable ID, author, severity,
disposition actor, reason, and evidence; external links are supplementary.
Allowed dispositions are: fixed with evidence; withdrawn by the finding's
author with a reason; or overridden by the deciding authority with a reason.
State honestly whether review was self-review, non-independent cold review, or
independent human review. The last round must cover the exact candidate named
in the decision. Later review/decision-only commits are allowed; a later change
to the proposal or prospective normative diff requires another round within the
limit.

## Decision

While Draft: `Pending`.

On disposition, record the deciding authority, date, pull-request decision URL,
outcome, exact candidate commit, exact normative files to land, compatibility
class, unresolved P0/P1 (must be none for Accepted), and any supersession links.
A disposition-only follow-up may change this section and Status to `Accepted`
or `Rejected`; it must not change the reviewed normative proposal.
